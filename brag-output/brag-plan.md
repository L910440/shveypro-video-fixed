# Brag Plan: ШвейПро

## What is this app?
A B2B custom sewing production landing page (bags, backpacks, textile goods made to order from 50 units) that runs in three languages — RU/EN/PT — so the same pitch works for clients abroad.

## The angle
"One factory, three languages, zero compromise." The site quietly proves it's not a local one-city shop: the same elegant terracotta-and-forest-green pitch, the same calculator, the same promise — just switch the language pill and the whole thing retypes itself. The hook is the language switch itself: RU → EN → PT, live, instant.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Extreme close-up on the header's three language pills (RU / EN / PT). A cursor taps EN. The pill goes solid white-on-dark. Behind it, the headline mid-retype.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero headline cross-dissolves through its three language states (RU → EN → PT) while the terracotta gradient bar underneath stays constant — the visual identity doesn't change, only the words do.
- The hero stat row ("от 50 шт / полный цикл / образец") locks into frame, each stat landing with a short beat — minimum order, full cycle, sample-before-batch.
- The cost calculator section: product dropdown (Сумка/Рюкзак/Шоппер...) cycles briefly, a price updates — "рассчитать стоимость" becomes a concrete number on screen, in the active language.

## Outro / punchline
Logo wordmark "ШвейПро" settles center, the three language pills reassemble underneath it RU · EN · PT, with the tagline line in whichever language the video is closing on. Punchline card: "Pick a language. We already speak it."

## User flow worth showing
None — landing-page only. Centerpiece is the language-switch interaction (closest thing this site has to a live flow) plus the calculator's dropdown → price moment.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, premium textile-atelier film — confident, not loud; the multilingual angle sells competence, not hype
- Interpretation: fewer scenes (3-4), longer holds, soft crossfades. No chaotic cuts — this is a serious B2B producer, not a startup joke. Motion should feel as considered as the product photography.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #F7F3EA (bg-milk) / #E9E0D2 (bg-light)
- Accent: #C86B4A (terracotta), gradient through #B25A3B / #97452B
- Text: #24483A (deep forest green, "--dark")
- Display font: Playfair Display (italic for emphasis words)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the language-pill switcher (RU/EN/PT) in the header, paired with the terracotta gradient underline used on the hero eyebrow

## Share copy (draft)
ШвейПро шьёт сумки и рюкзаки на заказ — и говорит об этом на трёх языках. RU · EN · PT, один калькулятор, один цех.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained corporate bed — confidence, not hype
- Music: soft, mid-tempo instrumental with a light swell on the outro; nothing percussive or chaotic
- Music treatment: starts quiet under the hook, gentle rise into the language cross-dissolve, settles for the calculator beat, soft swell + fade on the outro logo card
- Music cue guidance: to be detected at composition time (custom track, no bundled preset) — target one soft swell around the language cross-dissolve (~scene 2) and a final lift on the outro (~last 2s)
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — gradient underline glow may breathe very slightly with the music, nothing more
- SFX posture: sparse — one soft UI "tap" on the language-pill click in scene 1, one light "tick" per stat landing in scene 2, no SFX on outro beyond the music swell
- Audio-coupled moments: the language-pill tap (scene 1), the three stat landings (scene 2), the calculator price update (scene 3)
- Restraint rule: no chaotic layering, no beat-snapped text faster than the reading floor, no joke SFX (horns, cash registers, etc.) — this must read as a premium B2B reel

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The switch — 5s
Close crop on the header's three language pills. Cursor taps EN; the pill flips to solid white-on-dark (is-active state), the other two stay ghost-outlined. Behind/below, the hero eyebrow text ("Производство на заказ · для бизнеса") is visible mid-frame, about to retype.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated click on the EN pill, with the is-active state swap as the payoff
Audio intent: a single clean, confident tap — this is the "aha" moment
Audio-coupled idea: tap sound exactly on pill-state swap
Music: soft bed starts under this scene, very quiet
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — One identity, three languages — 6s
The hero headline and eyebrow cross-dissolve RU → EN → PT (three short holds, each readable: "Пошив сумок, рюкзаков..." → "Custom bags, backpacks..." → "Bolsas e mochilas sob encomenda..."). The terracotta gradient underline beneath the eyebrow stays lit and unchanging through all three — the brand doesn't move, only the words do. Then the three hero stats ("от 50 шт" / "полный цикл" / "образец") land one by one, each with a short settle.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 language states cross-dissolve in sequence, then 3 stat cards land one by one
Audio intent: a gentle rise under the language dissolves, a light tick on each stat landing
Audio-coupled idea: tick per stat arrival, synced to each card's settle, not its entrance
Music: bed rises slightly into this scene, one soft swell point
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The number — 5s
The calculator section: product dropdown briefly cycles two options (Сумка → Рюкзак) and the price output updates once, landing on a concrete number. Framed tight so the terracotta price figure and the "Рассчитать стоимость" CTA read clearly.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated dropdown change, price updates in sync
Audio intent: a quiet, precise "settle" — the number landing feels exact, not flashy
Audio-coupled idea: one soft tick exactly when the price digits update
Music: bed holds steady, no new swell yet
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
"ШвейПро" wordmark centers in Playfair Display italic on the bg-milk background. Below it, the three language pills reassemble side by side, RU · EN · PT, all three lit evenly this time (no single active state — the point is that all three are live). Punchline line settles underneath: "Говорим на вашем языке." (or the active-language equivalent).
Sequential/interaction: yes — three pills fade/settle in together as a set, not sequentially (this is the one spot sequential-per-item would undercut the "all equal" point)
Audio intent: the music's one real swell/lift, quiet confident close
Audio-coupled idea: none beyond the swell — let the line hold in silence after the music settles
Music: final swell + fade out
Transition mood: hold → end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, confident, understated corporate — think quiet piano/strings bed, not synth-pop
**Audio summary:** A soft, restrained bed that rises once through the language-switch moment and once more on the outro swell, framing two precise UI ticks (stat landing, price update) and a single tap on the language-pill click — nothing louder than the product itself.
